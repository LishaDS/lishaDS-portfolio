My background

I studied Electronics and Communication Engineering at St Joseph Engineering College, Mangaluru. My interests include FPGA design, digital systems, microelectronics, embedded technology, and practical applications of AI and image processing.

Through the Indian Naval Wing of the National Cadet Corps, I developed discipline, teamwork, communication, and the confidence to brief and represent my unit at national-level events.

“Build technology that works in the real world—and use leadership to make the impact bigger.”

Outside technical work, I enjoy singing, dancing, sports, and contributing to student communities and social-impact initiatives.
